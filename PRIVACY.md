# Privacy Policy — Pocket Preview

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Pocket Preview does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- When you click the extension icon, it reads the URL of the current tab (via the `activeTab` permission) for the sole purpose of re-opening that page in a phone-sized window.
- That URL is used locally, in your browser, at that moment only. It is never stored, logged, or sent anywhere.
- The extension has no servers, no analytics, no cookies, and no third-party code.
- It requests no host permissions and no access to your browsing history.

If you have any questions, contact: info@nourishedusa.org
